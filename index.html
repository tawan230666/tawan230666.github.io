BizFlow: QR table ordering

Diners scan a QR code at their table, order from their phone and pay with a PromptPay QR. The kitchen and cashier see every order change instantly.

02 Startup pitch: LandOpt

Beyond class projects, my team of three software engineering students took a product idea through RMUTL's University Business Incubator (UBI) startup programme in Chiang Mai, from problem research and a UI prototype to a five-minute pitch in front of a judging panel (February 2026).

PropTech · AI · Business model

TaxSave.AI: turning vacant-land tax into liquidity

The problem. Thai landowners who leave a plot unused pay a vacant-land tax that rises every three years, and selling costs about 3.3% in transfer tax. Investors, meanwhile, spend weeks paying people to check zoning before they buy. Most property sites list a price, but investors want to see the return.

Our solution. An AI land-use and tax simulator. The owner enters a title-deed number and the plot size, and the system:

  • Calculates the tax under current law and charts vacant against agricultural use
  • Analyses the location with GIS data to recommend the most valuable exit
  • Offers a one-click action: hire a partner to farm the plot (cutting the tax), or send a feasibility report to B2B investors

Business model. A 3% commission on deals, B2B SaaS subscriptions for developers and architects, and pay-per-use zoning feasibility reports. We asked for 200,000 THB in pre-seed funding.

What we built. A high-fidelity UI prototype covering the whole user journey, the rules of the Land and Building Tax Act turned into a data flow, and a system architecture ready for the MVP.
